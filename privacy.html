Clipbox Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The short version: Clipbox does not collect, transmit, sell or share your data. Everything you save stays on your iPhone.

What Clipbox stores

The items you save, your collections and rules, and your settings. This is stored only on your device, protected by iOS Data Protection. You can add Face ID / passcode protection with App Lock in Settings.

Network use

The only network request Clipbox makes is optional: when Fetch link titles & images is on (the default), Clipbox asks the website of a link you saved for its title and preview image, directly from your iPhone, using Apple's LinkPresentation framework. That request goes to that website — not to us. Clipbox never sends the *content* of your items anywhere, never fetches links that look like sign-in, password-reset, verification or other one-time links, and never fetches anything for items it treats as sensitive. You can turn this off in Settings → App Lock & privacy.

Clipboard

iOS does not allow any app to watch the clipboard. Clipbox reads the clipboard only when you tap the system Paste button, or if you turn on the optional "Save the clipboard when I open Clipbox" setting (iOS may ask your permission in that case).

Sensitive items

One-time codes, card numbers, keys and recovery phrases are always hidden in the app and never appear in widgets, Spotlight, Shortcuts results or notifications. One-time codes expire automatically (you choose when).

Analytics, advertising, tracking

None. Clipbox has no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs and asks for no account.

Extensions and Shortcuts

The Share extension, widgets and Shortcuts actions read and write the same on-device storage (an App Group container). Nothing leaves the device.

Purchases

Clipbox Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. Clipbox only learns whether you own it.

Your data, your control

Export everything at any time (JSON, CSV, Markdown, text, URLs) — always free. Delete everything with Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

Clipbox does not collect any data from anyone, including children.
