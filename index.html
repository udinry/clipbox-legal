Common questions

Why doesn't Clipbox save what I copy automatically? iOS doesn't let any app watch the clipboard in the background. Clipbox gives you one-gesture ways to save: the Share sheet, the Paste button, a Shortcut you can trigger with Back Tap or the Action Button, and widgets. See Settings → Quick capture in the app.

Is my data uploaded anywhere? No. Everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

How do I export or delete my data? Settings → Export (always free) and Settings → Delete all data.

What does Clipbox Pro include? A one-time purchase: unlimited items and collections, smart collections, rules and widgets. Free never locks the items you already have.

I bought Pro on another device. Settings → Restore purchases.
