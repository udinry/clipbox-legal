Save to Clipbox shortcut

One tap adds a ready-made shortcut to your Shortcuts app. It reads what you just copied and saves it to Clipbox. Then you can run it with Back Tap or the Action Button.

Add “Save to Clipbox” to Shortcuts

Then set up Back Tap

Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap → choose Save to Clipbox. Copy something anywhere, then double-tap the back of your iPhone.

Shortcuts downloads one small file from this page; nothing you save is sent anywhere.
